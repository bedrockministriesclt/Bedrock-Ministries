What margin
made possible.

These stories come directly from families who have lived with Bedrock and experienced what financial margin made possible in their own lives.

Colin and Cami Crook

Twenty four months of intention.

Life has a way of moving quickly from the moment it starts, and for many of us, debt enters the picture early on. The idea of “getting ahead” is all to often unrealistic. What we were fortunate enough to experience was the power of 24 months of intention early in our marriage and ministry careers. We came home from our honeymoon to a Bedrock rental where affordable rent, on entry-level ministry salaries, allowed us to tackle debt and begin saving—something far from the norm for most. Beyond housing, we were challenged to get specific about the goals we wanted to work toward early on, even if it would be years before we saw them take shape. Two years later, we purchased a townhome in an area ideal for us. As we write this testimony, we are weeks away from welcoming our first child and are experiencing the incredible reality of freedom from crippling debt. Beyond establishing strong financial habits, Bedrock helped us learn how to slow life down just enough to be intentional about what matters most and where we’re headed. We hope it does the same for you!

Juan and Rachel Rodriguez

A life changing impact.

Bedrock made such a life-changing impact on our family! With our financial set up and jobs in ministry, we never thought we’d be able to afford living in the city. Bedrock not only made it possible for us to be in our first single-family home, we were also able to live in one of the most desirable locations in Charlotte. In the one year renting from this ministry, we paid off close to $10,000 in debt! Thank you for providing a space where our kids could grow, our faith could be strengthened, and our financial goals could be attainable. Bedrock has marked our family forever!

Lex and Tiff Bibbs

From Bedrock to homeownership.

We’re the Bibbs family—Lex and Tiff—and we are incredibly thankful for the opportunity to live in the Skyview home through Bedrock's generous vision to support couples like us. That experience helped us save for a down payment, pay off debt, and prepare for homeownership. Even more than that, we felt encouraged and supported every step of the way. In 2023, we closed on our first home in Dallas, NC, and we are forever grateful to Bedrock for helping make that dream possible.

Justin and Hannah Lewis

The lower rent made room to move.

My wife and I were determined to get serious about paying down our debt, but we struggled to find areas in our budget where we could realistically cut back. With rent being high and a baby on the way, we were stretched pretty thin.

Bedrock stepped in and gave us an incredible opportunity by allowing us to live in one of their houses. The lower rent combined with the extra accountability made all the difference. Because of their generosity, we were able to pay off $4,000 in credit card debt and put an additional $3,000 toward our car loan!

The common thread

The house is the vehicle. The goal is a stronger financial foundation.

Every household enters with a different story. Bedrock creates space, then residents do the work of budgeting, reducing debt, building savings, and preparing for what comes next.
