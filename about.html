Why Bedrock
exists.

Bedrock was built around a simple conviction: financial pressure should not keep people from saying yes to the work they feel called to do.

Why Bedrock began

Josh and Angie kept encountering people who felt called to ministry but were carrying debt and facing housing costs that made the numbers difficult to sustain.

That tension became personal. They chose to use housing as a practical way to create financial margin, and later kept a property dedicated to the mission so another family could have the same opportunity.

Our Mission

We exist to put church staff and ministry workers on a path to financial freedom.

Bedrock combines housing support with intentional financial goals. Residents continue paying rent, participate in the process, and direct the margin created toward the future they are working to build.

What guides Bedrock

Dignity

Residents are partners in the work. They still pay rent, make the decisions, and own the progress.

Intentionality

Financial margin has a purpose. Goals, budgets, and accountability help turn extra room into forward movement.

Stewardship

Homes, grants, donor support, and resident effort are treated as resources to steward carefully.
