Why your support matters

For people serving in ministry, housing costs can take up more of the monthly budget than is sustainable. Bedrock helps lower that pressure so residents can intentionally direct money toward debt reduction, savings, and future housing goals.

How your gift creates margin

You give

Bedrock supports

Margin opens

Families move forward

Residents use that room to work toward goals they identified before moving in.

What your gift supports

Housing support

Bedrock helps make rent sustainable for households in the program while residents continue paying their portion.

Property care

Giving supports repairs, maintenance, insurance, taxes, yard care, and the practical costs required to keep homes safe and ready.

Future capacity

Support also helps Bedrock build toward serving more families through future housing opportunities.

Why monthly giving matters

A Bedrock journey can span multiple years. Monthly giving helps create the consistency needed to keep families in the program long enough to make meaningful progress.

One-time gifts matter too. Recurring support gives Bedrock a stronger foundation for the long term.
