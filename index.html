Our Mission

We exist to put church staff and ministry workers on a path to financial freedom.

Housing is one of the largest expenses in a household budget. Bedrock uses affordable housing as a practical way to create margin, giving residents room to reduce debt, build savings, and prepare for greater financial stability.

Why Bedrock began

Josh and Angie kept meeting people who felt called to ministry but could not afford to take the job because of debt, housing costs, and limited financial margin.

Housing became a practical way to help change that equation. Bedrock creates room for people to work toward financial stability while continuing to serve.

What guides Bedrock

Margin has value when it is used with intention. These commitments shape how Bedrock serves residents and stewards support.

Dignity

Residents are partners in the work. They continue paying rent, make the decisions, and own the progress.

Intentionality

Financial margin has a purpose. Goals, budgets, and accountability help turn extra room into forward movement.

Stewardship

Homes, grants, donor support, and resident effort are treated as resources to steward carefully.

How Bedrock creates margin

Housing is the starting point. The purpose is greater financial stability.

Housing

Bedrock helps bring housing costs to a sustainable level for people serving in ministry.

Margin

Lower housing costs create additional room in a household's monthly budget.

Purpose

Residents set financial goals and intentionally direct that margin toward them.

Progress

Debt can decrease, savings can grow, and families can prepare for greater stability.

What margin can make possible

Lex and Tiff used their time with Bedrock to pay down debt, save toward a down payment, and prepare for homeownership. In 2023, they purchased their first home.
